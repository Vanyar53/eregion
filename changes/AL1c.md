### Fixed
- **A card of the control plane or of an identity is filed on what it is about, never on a VM it has nothing to do with** (AL1c, #597 ; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La carte se range sur ce qu'elle vise » ; `docs/design/console.md`, « Les cartes »). Measured on the bench by ID1: six cards filed on the first VM online, none about it. The RulePoller now reads the target in the row (`glorfindel/card_target.py`) and the card keeps it (`target`): an NSG rule on the NSG, shown on each VM it covers that the discovery knows, an offline one included; a snapshot or a download access on the VM of the source disk — the row names the snapshot only, the source is read on Azure while the snapshot exists (`ReadersMixin.control_topology`, read only, cached), else the resource group, the limit said; a role on its scope (the VM, else the group; the subscription, the control plane); anything else on its resource group, or the control plane. A row routed by the address that made the call (`CallerIpAddress`: an NSG written from a watched VM) is filed on what it writes, the calling VM named as the caller (« appel depuis vm-… »), never attributed to it; only a row that names the VM itself (a role on it) is that VM's; a call from a VM the rule does not watch no longer drops the row (« appel depuis une VM que la règle ne surveille pas »). An identity card is filed on the identity (`entra:<type>:<object id>`), never a VM. What the row and Azure could not say is written on the card — only what was read: a resource missing from a listing is « deleted, or out of the watch's rights », never « deleted » alone; a VM the rule does not watch is said so (« absente de la découverte, exclue de la règle, ou d'un autre backend »), never « unknown to the discovery ». A rule of the control plane or of an identity speaks with no VM watched (every VM excluded, an empty discovery just after a start): excluding every VM no longer silences it. Unchanged: no action, no model, never merged, no incident opened or fed, nothing in the memory; the data plane keeps its anchor.
