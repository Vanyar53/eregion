### Changed
- **A brute force that succeeded, then root on the same VM: the decision proposes at least `isolate_vm`** (B3a, #609; `docs/glorfindel.md`, `docs/design/correlation.md`). The CD harness of H0 drew `block_suspicious_ip` one time in three on its paired case (a brute force after a sudo to root on the same VM), above the gate: the source blocked, the VM where root was taken left standing. A deterministic floor in `decide`, never in the prompt: when the correlation (COR1) marked a brute force with a login from its address (`ssh-login-after-brute-force`) and a sudo to root (T1548.003) was detected on the same VM — in either order, kept by the VM's incident (`Incident.confirmed`, the new `confirm_facts` node) — the action is raised to `isolate_vm`, whatever the model chose; a model that already isolates keeps its choice. A brute force alone, a sudo alone, two VMs, failures the correlation never saw succeed or logins it could not check keep the model's choice; so does any signal that is not a detection (a `recovery_complete`, a timeout). Every gate then judges the isolation as the model's own: the confidence, the signal, the attribution, the telemetry, the autonomy mode (in `human_only` nothing runs, the card proposes `isolate_vm`) and the agreement of the draws (each confirming draw floored alike). An action only a human approves (a restore) is never lost: the VM is contained first, then a `destructive_action` card proposes it; an escalated cycle names it in its reason. The card, the notification, the `account_compromised` card and the debug line say why the floor played, in words that fit the choice set aside. Known limits: the incident's TTL slides with every signal of the VM and `confirmed` is never cleared, so while the incident lives every later detection on the VM is raised; in `respond`, a poll longer than the TTL can lose a fact for the next detection.
  - The paired case 2/2 of the harness has failures only (no COR1 mark): the floor does not play there, and it stays as it is, a measure of the model alone. A new harness case, kind `floor` (`scripts/llm_smoke.py`, Jonathan's decision, 06/11), is the same brute force marked by COR1, its incident holding the confirmed sudo: `isolate_vm` expected, 3/3 whatever the model draws — the floor proven end to end in the CD.
