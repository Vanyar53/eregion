### Added
- **The bench's client layer sets the azurerm canary's runs aside from `nsg-change-outside-glorfindel`, by its group and its principal together** (CAN2, #605; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Le canari azurerm »). The scheduled `canary-azurerm` run creates then deletes its throwaway NSGs (`nsg-customer-<suffix>`, `nsg-glorfindel-quarantine-canary-<suffix>`) in `rg-eregion-canary` under its own OIDC principal (P-04): twelve cards on H0, judged right (Jonathan's decision, 06/11). Of the shipped `source: azure_activity` rules, only `nsg-change-outside-glorfindel` reads what the canary does (held by a test). `infra/glorfindel-host/client_rules.bench.yaml` holds that rule copied as it is (`base_sha256`, WL1's drift), plus one exclusion, read on the operation's latest line: the group is `rg-eregion-canary` (`ResourceGroup` and `_ResourceId`, without case, as ARM reads them) **and** the caller is the canary's principal (its appid in `Claims.appid` or as `Caller`, and no user's name as the caller). Another caller in that group, a user signed in through the canary's application, that principal in another group: a card, as before. `make canary-jit` on a workstation runs under the operator's session: its runs keep their cards. The shipped base is unchanged.

- **A client rule may take a GUID from the watch's environment, and nothing else** (CAN2). The canary's appid never enters the repository: the copy declares `env_guids: [EREGION_RULE_CANARY_APPID]` and writes `${EREGION_RULE_CANARY_APPID}` in its query. At load (`load_config`, after the drift and before the layers merge), the watch puts in place of that placeholder — and of it alone — the value of its environment variable, checked to be a GUID and written lowercase: no other character ever reaches the query (no KQL injection). Only `EREGION_RULE_…` variables, named in a list by the rule that takes them (else the entry is skipped and said); any other `${…}` stays as written, as every query since SEC2. Absent, empty or no GUID: the copy is set aside and the shipped rule of its name runs — more cards, never fewer — and it is said (a note at the watch's start and under `glorfindel rules`, the variable named, never its value); if that shipped rule would not load, the client rule runs with its placeholder left as written (it equals no identifier), and that is said too. `docker-compose.yml` passes `EREGION_RULE_CANARY_APPID` to the watch and the console (which shows the rules as the watch loads them); `infra/glorfindel-host/host_env.py` copies it from the operator's environment, and `--check` says a value that is no GUID, never refusing. `make glorfindel-host-client-rules` reads such a rule back with a stand-in GUID (H0's appid is never on the workstation) and says H0's watch needs the variable. Jonathan's gesture, once a release with CAN2 is deployed: `infra/glorfindel-host/README.md`, « La couche du client du banc ».
