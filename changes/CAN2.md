- **A client rule may take a GUID from the watch's environment, and nothing else** (CAN2). The canary's appid never enters the repository: the copy declares `env_guids: [EREGION_RULE_CANARY_APPID]` and writes `${EREGION_RULE_CANARY_APPID}` in its query. At load (`load_config`, after the drift and before the layers merge), the watch puts in place of that placeholder — and of it alone — the value of its environment variable, checked to be a GUID and written lowercase: no other character ever reaches the query (no KQL injection). Only `EREGION_RULE_…` variables, named in a list by the rule that takes them (else the entry is skipped and said); any other `${…}` stays as written, as every query since SEC2. Absent, empty or no GUID: the copy is set aside and the shipped rule of its name runs — more cards, never fewer — and it is said (a note at the watch's start and under `glorfindel rules`, the variable named, never its value); if that shipped rule would not load, the client rule runs with its placeholder left as written (it equals no identifier), and that is said too. `docker-compose.yml` passes `EREGION_RULE_CANARY_APPID` to the watch and the console (which shows the rules as the watch loads them); `infra/glorfindel-host/host_env.py` copies it from the operator's environment, and `--check` says a value that is no GUID, never refusing. `make glorfindel-host-client-rules` reads such a rule back with a stand-in GUID (H0's appid is never on the workstation) and says H0's watch needs the variable. Jonathan's gesture, once a release with CAN2 is deployed: `infra/glorfindel-host/README.md`, « Le canari azurerm (CAN2) » — the appid written by the shell into H0's `host.env` from the exact filter `az ad app list --filter "displayName eq 'eregion-canary'"` (never copied from the screen), one line and one GUID checked, cross-checked against the `CallerAppId` of a canary card of 06/11; then `docker compose up -d watch war-room` on H0 (a `restart` does not reread `.env`), then `make glorfindel-host-client-rules`; at the canary's next scheduled run (06:00 UTC), no card of the canary.
