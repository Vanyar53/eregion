### Changed
- **The release gate's shards are cut on the durations measured** (CI6d, #545; `docs/operations.md`, « La porte de release »). The gate's first two runs (05/11) took 22 to 60 min a shard in one, 26 to 72 in the other: the estimate weighed every mutant 1 (8 on an invariant machine) and each unmutated run 1, when `tests/unit/test_host_cd.py`'s 97 mutants alone made a quarter of the whole (about 280 s each) and the unmutated runs a third of a shard.
  - A shard's `--json` now says each mutant's measure (its copy included) and each target's unmutated run, in seconds (`seconds`, `targets`); the rows and `--total` are unchanged.
  - `scripts/mutants/durations.json` keeps them, at the gate's budget (500 sequences, 2 rounds): `python scripts/mutation_check.py --update-durations DIR…` reads a run's artifacts (those from before CI6d too, their seconds in the rows), keeps each smallest measure (a slower runner slows a whole shard alike), only the mutants and targets of this tree, only results at that budget; nothing else. Seeded from runs 41997986094 and 42009742778 (2 851 mutants; the unmutated runs, measured from the next run on).
  - `shard_of` weighs a mutant by its measure (unmeasured: its target's median, else the old estimate, 13 s, eight times that on an invariant machine) and a piece by its mutants plus its target's unmutated run (unmeasured: one round of its slowest mutant, at least the estimate). A target is cut only when its mutants weigh more than a quarter of a shard's share, never by a count. Still deterministic — the same tree and file, the same shards — and each mutant in exactly one shard. Each shard's pools now take the heaviest first.
  - `python scripts/mutation_check.py --plan 14` says each shard's estimated time, measuring nothing.
  - Estimated on the measures: the heaviest of the 14 shards about 41 min on an ordinary runner (36 to 41), the control set's about 31, for 60 and 72 measured before. A slow runner is not caught up by any cut: the slowest measured on 05/11 (1.77 times) would take about 72 min; nothing indivisible stands in the way (the longest mutant, 21 min; the longest unmutated run, about 10).
  - The Monday measure (`.github/workflows/mutants.yml`) runs in the gate's shards: 14 of the mutants, 2 of the control set, then their total at the dispatch's budget, held to `min_caught` — 17 jobs, its actions the gate's, pinned by commit. On one runner it outlasted its 180 min bound.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_mutation_shards.py`, `tests/unit/test_workflows.py`. Mutants M3790–M3799 (`scripts/mutants/CI6d.py`).
