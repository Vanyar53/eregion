### Changed
- **The mutants are red as soon as one survives outside the known survivors** (CI6e, #553; `docs/design/preuves.md`, « Rouge dès qu'un mutant survit hors de la liste connue »). The release gate and the Monday measure went red below `min_caught`, 14 of about 2 850 mutants: it held almost nothing, and six survivors had no written reason.
  - A known survivor is a mutant whose reason opens with a mark the harness reads, then a colon (`KNOWN_MARKS`): `EQUIVALENT` and `MULTI-HOST`, not counted; `TIMING`, counted and known. `STALE` stays uncounted.
  - Any counted mutant that survives a round (`SURVIVES`, `flaky`, `FIXED-ONLY`) without such a reason makes `--total` exit 1, said with its id and its title; so does a run of its own (`--only`, without `--json`). A shard (`--json`) writes and says it, and exits 0: its result is always uploaded, the total holds the gate. A known survivor caught in every round is said, never red, so that its reason is taken off by hand. The control set is said, never held.
  - `--min-caught` is gone, refused if given (exit 2): the harness and the workflows always come from one tree, and a flag kept without effect would hide that it holds nothing. `.github/workflows/mutants.yml` loses its `min_caught` input; both workflows' totals call `--total` alone.
  - `scripts/mutant_anchors.py` names a reason whose mark the harness does not read as a fault: its mutant would be taken for an unknown survivor; so is a `TIMING` reason that names no race test. A `TIMING` survivor caught in no round of a run is said apart by the total, never red.
  - A mutant gone `STALE` no longer passes in silence: `tests/unit/test_mutant_anchors.py` checks every mutant of the tree applies, in the ordinary suite (under a second). A meta-test, never a killer: in a copy the harness mutates, the mutant applied has left its own text, so the harness sets `EREGION_MUTANT_RUN=1` in each run of a copy and the test skips there, saying why — else it would catch M3186, M3585, and any mutant whose target holds it, by construction.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_mutation_shards.py`, `tests/unit/test_mutant_anchors.py`, `tests/unit/test_workflows.py`. Mutants M3915–M3924, M3965–M3968, M4020, M4021 (`scripts/mutants/CI6e.py`).
