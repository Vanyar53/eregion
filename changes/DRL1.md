### Added
- **The weekly bench drill, designed: one exercise a week, played by a timer on Orodruin** (DRL1, #613; `docs/design/drill-banc.md`, indexed in `CLAUDE.md`). Jonathan's decision of 06/11, so that the age of the last proof (under 30 days) holds by itself. The design, reviewed once in Opus 5.5 and corrected, covers:
  - **The content.** A shipped drill, `drills/banc-hebdo.yaml`, with one new block, `schedule` (shipped drills only, every wire in zone `bench`, techniques implemented, non-destructive, from a public library). Version 1 holds one wire: T1136.001, the pinned Atomic Red Team test already proven from Orodruin. What the watch should see lives apart, in `expectations/banc-hebdo.yaml`, which the injectors' image never holds. The drill's digest covers its whole file.
  - **The timer.** A systemd timer, installed and enabled by the operator's arming, never before. Three inputs decide an occurrence: the verified image, the arming file and the clock. What comes from H0 or the channel (the watch's heartbeat, the arming permit) can only refuse. It runs in zone `bench` only, judged again before each wire. It runs at most once in six days, its registry read and written under Orodruin's lock, the lock handed down to the runner. It is never caught up, and never again after an emergency stop until re-armed (a permit, below). It goes through the same judge as a Dagorlad request. A stopped target is started by the drill itself, after the contract, the watch and the journal, its isolation read first. The time budget is set out (`RuntimeMaxSec` 1 h), and the stop of the service closes the windows.
  - **The emergency stop.** An arming permit, closed by default: the operator writes it in the channel when arming, the kill and the disarming remove it, and an occurrence requires it, equal to the local arming. The channel's retention can only let it expire, which closes. The kill also disables the scheduled start of Orodruin right after the revocation, and marks the drill disarmed, so that H0 raises no false absence alarm. On a stop, the service relays SIGTERM to the running wire and waits for it, and the wire's window lasts no longer than its time left.
  - **The report.** Each occurrence is judged on H0, which also raises the alarm when nothing arrived, only when the permit and its own copy of the drill agree (else "re-arm expected"). The Banc session reads the workspace as an independent witness, the rule's relative time filters rewritten onto the occurrence's window.
  - **The rest.** The bias of the last-proof indicator (one technique keeps it green; rotation comes next), the cost (how Orodruin is powered on, and the narrow role of its scheduled start), the threat model frontier it adds (proposed, not yet written there; the public schedule said), and what VP1 reads.
  - **Jonathan's decisions of 06/11**, recorded in its §10: the scheduled start, Tuesday 19:00 UTC, T1136.001 alone, alerts by Glorfindel's bot, the digest over the whole drill file, the kill disabling the scheduled start, the public schedule, gondolin left in its real mode.
