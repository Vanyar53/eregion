### Fixed
- **The CD's tests no longer send H0 back on a wall clock set back under load** (FL1, #540). The health compares the watch's beat (a file's mtime) with `since`, a `time.time()` taken before the switch; in the tests the fake switch is quick (the two readings as little as 14 ms apart, measured), the fake watch beats once, and `CLOCK_REALTIME` is set back (WSL2: about 65 ms every 30 s, measured) while the kernel stamps a file with its coarse clock (up to 25 ms behind `time.time()`): the beat read as from before the switch, « la watch n'a pas battu depuis la bascule », and five tests failed at random under load. The agent and the fake watch now share, in these tests only, one wall clock that never steps back (the system's monotonic clock from a base): the agent runs as on H0 (`python eregion_cd.py <mode>`, through a launcher that sets that clock), the fake watch stamps its beat on it. The CD is unchanged; the oracle stays « a beat after the switch ». Measured: a step of 65 ms made synthetic fails 85 of the file's 287 tests before, none after.
