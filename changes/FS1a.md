### Added
- **The signed action queue: its format and its verification, with no change of behaviour** (FS1a, #551, first lot of FS1, #413; `docs/design/file-actions-signee.md`, §7). Nobody reads the queue yet to act: the console and the bot keep their direct call, no queue is picked up, no request is written.
  - **`eregion_core/action_queue.py`**: a request of a service (the console, the bot) — exactly its fields, at most 4 KiB, signed by the service's own Ed25519 key under `eregion.action-request/1`; the proposal the watch signs on a card under `eregion.action-queue/1` (its `kind` signed: a proposal never reads as a receipt or a status, which FS1b signs under the same domain); the closed list of verbs (`VERBS`, each service's in `SERVICE_VERBS`: the console all, the bot `reset` only; open to extension by these constants alone, no restore) and of refusals (`REFUSALS`, each with its reason; `CRITICAL`).
  - **`verify_request`**, a pure check with no network and no call to the cloud, from the cheapest to the dearest: read bounded and never through a link, the exact form (a field twice refused too), a signature, the key pinned for this directory (`EREGION_QUEUE_KEYS`, the watch's environment only), the signature that holds, the service, the time (more than 30 s ahead: `not_yet_valid`; a life over 120 s: `too_long`; from `expires_at`: `expired`), the nonce (claimed before anything runs; a register that cannot be read or written refuses), the verb and the rate (30 a minute per service). `proposal_refusal`: the card still waits, carries the proposal this watch signed, unchanged, the one the request names, for its verb, target and address, never executed.
  - **The services' keys are drawn by the watch's own code**: `watch_key.load_or_create(…, name=…)` draws the console's and the bot's key (`~/.eregion/console/queue_ed25519`, `~/.eregion/bot/queue_ed25519`) under the same rules as the watch's — one draw, two uses. The watch's key, its place, its messages and what it signs are unchanged, byte for byte (a known signature computed before FS1a). No service draws its key yet (FS1b).
  - **The watch signs a proposal on each card it writes that holds an action a human may run** (a type approved in one click with its action, or `verification_failed` for its reset), with its SEC19 key and counter, signed again when the card is seen again. A card the console, the bot or the CLI writes carries none; a key unavailable never stops a card.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_action_queue.py`, `tests/unit/test_action_proposal.py`; mutants M3865–M3879 (`scripts/mutants/FS1a.py`).
