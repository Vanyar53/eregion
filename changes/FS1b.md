### Added
- **The watch picks the signed action queue up, and the console and the bot deposit in it — behind `EREGION_ACTION_QUEUE=1` only** (FS1b, #563, second lot of FS1, #413; `docs/design/file-actions-signee.md`, §8). The flag is false by default: without it nothing changes — no queue is read, no request written, the console and the bot keep their direct call (a test holds it for each of the three).
  - **The watch's queue** (`glorfindel/queue_watch.py`): a thread of its own, never the watch's loop nor its heartbeat, reads `runs/console/queue/` and `runs/bot/queue/` every second (16 requests per service and per pass at most). Each request: its name must be its nonce; a receipt `received` first; then the verification of FS1a; its card's proposal that this watch signed, not consumed — or, for a verb without a card, what the watch holds (`nothing_to_undo`); **the console's guards replayed by the same code** (`glorfindel/approval.py`: the one-click approval, its age read from the proposal's signed `issued_at`, never from `last_seen`, a restore after the card; one host address out of `never_block`, a reach of `vm` or `subnet`; the VM in the watch's perimeter; the block of this IP on this VM; the human a bot's request names, read against the watch's own copy of `DISCORD_ALLOWED_USERS` and `DISCORD_ALLOWED_ROLES`); a read-only watch refuses; the proposal consumed (one proposal, one execution, a failed one included); the receipt `accepted`; the execution in a worker, under `_vm_lock`, with the watch's functions (the CLI's release and reset moved to `glorfindel/gestures.py`, which the CLI calls too), checked after (`verification_failed` card when not `True`), the card settled; `done` or `failed`.
  - **Receipts and status signed by the watch** (`runs/glorfindel/queue/<service>-<id>.json`, `status.json`: the posture, the keys pinned, the refusals counted) under `eregion.action-queue/1`. A forged, replayed, foreign-key or other-service request makes a critical card (`queue_tampered`), decided by the code, no action, at most one per service and per hour; an unreadable register, a `queue_unavailable` card.
  - **The console's write routes** (approve, release, reset, unblock, extend to subnet, snapshot; `eregion_console/action_requests.py`) deposit a request signed by the console's key and answer from the receipt (`via: "queue"`); **the bot's Revert** deposits `reset` bound to its `verification_failed` card. Each service draws its key at its start with the flag (`~/.eregion/console/queue_ed25519`, `~/.eregion/bot/queue_ed25519`); `make queue-keys` and `make glorfindel-host-queue-keys` give the line to pin at the watch (`EREGION_QUEUE_KEYS`) — prepared, not to be run before FS1c.
  - **A new writer of `runs/`**, `bot/` (its requests); the watch mounts it read-only, the bot mounts `runs/glorfindel/queue` read-only (that subdirectory only) and `~/.eregion/bot`; the watch mounts `~/.eregion/watch-queue` (its register) and receives `EREGION_QUEUE_KEYS` and the bot's allowed lists. `make glorfindel-start` and H0's `deploy.sh` make `~/.eregion/bot`, `~/.eregion/watch-queue` and `runs/glorfindel/queue` as the operator first.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_action_queue_watch.py` (end to end on the in-memory Azure, each refusal, a replay, a forged card, another service's key, the flag absent, an unreadable register, a stop between the claim and the execution, the console's and the bot's deposits, the mounts); mutants M3975–M4014 (`scripts/mutants/FS1b.py`).

### Changed
- **The watch's register of the action queue is written durably and bounds a service's claims** (FS1b, #563; `docs/design/file-actions-signee.md`, §7). A nonce claimed, and a proposal consumed (`consumed.json`, new), are fsync'd with their directory before the watch answers; a service's rate is counted before its nonce is claimed, so a compromised service key never fills the register; an unreadable register refuses whatever it holds, and never raises.
- **A card seen again keeps its proposal while it stands and is younger than 10 min** (FS1b, #563): re-signed at each refresh, its digest changed under a request just named for it (`proposal_mismatch`).
- **The console imports the queue's list of one-click approvable cards and calls the shared guards** (`action_queue.APPROVABLE_TYPES`, `glorfindel/approval.py`) instead of its own copies; their behaviour is unchanged (a card dated without its zone now reads « sans date lisible » instead of failing). A restore record of the VM there but unreadable (a list, a date without its zone, a field missing, a file that cannot be read) now refuses the one-click approval, « Restore illisible : agir depuis la CLI » — it used to fail the route, or read as no restore.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_action_queue.py`, `tests/unit/test_action_proposal.py`, `tests/unit/test_dependency_failures.py`.
