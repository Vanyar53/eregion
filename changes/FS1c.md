### Security
- **The bot no longer writes the console's directory** (FS1c, #571; `docs/design/modele-de-menace.md`). Its gestures go to `runs/bot/` (`manual_actions.jsonl`; the CLI it runs carries `EREGION_RUNS_WRITER=bot`) and it no longer mounts `runs/console`: a compromised bot can no longer replace or drown the console's requests of the signed action queue, nor make a critical card blame the console. The console mounts `runs/bot` read-only; the feed, the overview's lifts and the containment analysis read both hands.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_action_queue_async.py`, `tests/unit/test_runs_mounts.py`, `tests/unit/test_profiles.py`.

### Changed
- **The console refuses a fifth gesture in flight, without the flag too — visible on H0 at the next release** (FS1c, #571; `docs/design/console.md`). Its write routes (approve, release, reset, unblock, extend to subnet, snapshot) run in an executor of their own, 4 gestures at once: past them, a click is answered `503` (`busy`: « 4 gestures already in flight in this console: try again once one ends ») at once, nothing done. A gesture takes its one slot at its entry and holds it to its end: once accepted, its action, its check and its note always run — never « busy » after the isolation was made.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_action_queue_async.py`.

### Added
- **The signed action queue, asynchronous — behind `EREGION_ACTION_QUEUE=1`, still off by default** (FS1c, #571; `docs/design/file-actions-signee.md` §9, `docs/design/console.md`). A write route of the console deposits its request and answers at once with its id; `GET /api/queue/<id>` says its receipt (`waiting`, `received`, `accepted`, then the route's former answer, or `not_taken`, `untrusted`, `unknown`), and no thread of the console waits for the watch. The gesture is noted once, by whichever read settles it — a page closed before its receipt included. The bot's Revert says each state of its receipt in the VM's thread, holding no thread, and is noted once in `runs/bot/manual_actions.jsonl` from its receipt. The write buttons are greyed by the status the watch signs (missing, stale, read-only, another key pinned), the reason said.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_action_queue_async.py`, `tests/unit/test_action_queue_watch.py`.
- **The switch prepared, not made** (FS1c, #571; `docs/operations.md`, « La mesure au banc »). `make glorfindel-host-queue-keys` draws each service's key in its own container (`pin <service> --draw`) before the flag; `host_env.py` never copies this workstation's queue variables, and `--check` refuses the flag without the console's key (or the bot's while it runs); the watch says each missing key at its start. The order of Jonathan's gestures for the bench measure: the keys, the flag, one approval end to end, the way back.
