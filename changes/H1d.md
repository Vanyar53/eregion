### Fixed
- **The CD's health judges the watch's heartbeat by its identity, no longer by the wall clock** (H1d, #549; `infra/glorfindel-host/README.md`, « Le CD de H0 »). The health asked the beat's mtime to be no older than `since`, a `time.time()` taken before the switch: two readings of the wall clock. A clock set back during the health (about 120 s or more) read the new watch's beats as from before the switch and sent H0 back; and the old watch could beat in the few seconds before the switch stopped it — killed by a SIGKILL, its beat stayed, and a new watch that never beat passed for healthy. The CD now takes the beat (its inode, its mtime in ns) once `deploy.sh --activate` has returned — the old watch stopped — and asks another one: the watch rewrites the file by a rename (SEC17), each beat a new file. The way back and a pass taken up (the release's watch already running) take it the same way. Taken before the switch, as first thought, the old watch's last beat would still pass: measured by a mutant. A healthy watch beats at its start, then about every minute: when its first beat lands before the switch returns, the health waits for the next one. `since` stays for the journals read (V-34).
  - **The agent changes** (`eregion_cd.py` is one of its `AGENT_FILES`): the release that carries it is deployed by the agent before, which still judges the beat by the wall clock; then `#deploy` asks for `make glorfindel-host-cd-install`, from an up-to-date checkout of `main`, then `make glorfindel-host-cd-check`.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_host_cd.py` (the fake watch now beats as the real one, by a rename, at its start and at each look of the health; a clock set back 120 s during the switch; the old watch's last beat with a silent new one, on the switch, the way back and a pass taken up).
