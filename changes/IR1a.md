### Added
- **Every Entra ID identity card carries its plan, and a Verify button that reads Microsoft Graph again, read only** (IR1a, #552; `docs/design/reponse-identite.md` §3.2, `docs/glorfindel.md`, `docs/design/console.md`). The plan is written by the code from the log's row, never by the model: the identity executor's verbs in their order (remove the added credential, or the one an unusual sign-in used; disable the consent's client application, never the resource's principal), then the steps for a human (remove a grant, an app role assignment or a directory role assignment; revoke a user's sessions; renew the other credentials), each with its exact ids read on Graph (the object, the keyId, the grant's or the assignment's id), its complete command, what to keep before, its side effect and its way back. A parameter neither in the row nor read is marked « not read » and its command said incomplete, never invented; nothing of the log that is not an id reaches a command. Glorfindel's own identity gets no executor verb, its credentials renewed first; an identity of IDI1's `never_target` list turns a verb into a human's step. Verify: the console writes the request on the card (it never reads Graph), the watch reads Graph and notes held, not held or unreadable on the card's thread — never held on a right missing, an error or a delay. Without a Graph right (H0 today) the plan keeps what the log gives and Verify says unreadable; the read permissions to grant the watch are in `docs/operations.md` (« La lecture de Microsoft Graph par la watch »). No executor exists yet (IR1c): every item is done by hand.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_identity_plan.py` (a fake Graph, `tests/unit/fake_graph.py`), `tests/unit/test_console_identity_plan.py`; mutants M3880–M3899.

### Changed
- **`IDENTITY_ACTIONS`, the closed list of the seven identity verbs** (IR1a, #552; `glorfindel/response/policy.py`): disjoint from `AUTONOMOUS_ACTIONS`, within `HUMAN_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`. The model never reads them: the prompt names `MODEL_GATED_ACTIONS`, the gated actions a decision may name, and is byte for byte what it was. An identity decision keeps `action: ""`, and both locks of C2 hold.
