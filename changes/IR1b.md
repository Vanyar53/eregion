### Security
- **The identity executor's contract, read at its start: armed only within a closed list of rights, else passive and it refuses to start** (IR1b, #572; `docs/design/reponse-identite.md` §3.4, `docs/operations.md`, « L'exécuteur d'identité »). `glorfindel/providers/azure/identity_contract.py` reads the executor's own Graph token first, with no call (an application's token, of the declared tenant and application; its `roles` and `wids` within the list), then its rights again on Microsoft Graph, read only: its application permissions, each named by its resource (Microsoft Graph only), its directory role assignments, active (each at its one scope) and eligible (none), and what it owns (applications and their principals only — never itself, never an identity of `never_target`: owning one is acting as it). The closed list: `Application.ReadWrite.OwnedBy` and `User.RevokeSessions.All`; Directory Readers at the tenant, User Administrator at the bench's administrative unit only (its id pinned), Attribute Assignment Reader at Eregion's attribute set only. Any other right is `beyond_contract` — a refused one (`RoleManagement.ReadWrite.Directory`, `Application.ReadWrite.All`, `Directory.ReadWrite.All`… Global Administrator, Privileged Role Administrator…) named so; a contract it cannot read is `contract_unreadable`: passive both, and `start` raises `Passive`. A role is known by its definition's id, never by its name nor by the `templateId` a custom role may set; a permission is held only when the token and the assignments both say so; a right it lacks takes its verbs away (`Verdict.allows`) without disarming it. Its own certificate only, never `DefaultAzureCredential` (on H0 the VM's token endpoint is the watch's). `glorfindel identity-contract` says the verdict, exit 0 only if armed. No verb is written: IR1c.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_identity_contract.py` (the fake Graph of IR1a, `tests/unit/fake_graph.py`, now with owned objects); mutants M4070–M4084.
