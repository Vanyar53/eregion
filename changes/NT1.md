### Fixed
- **The hosted watch reads the never_target list: an identity card's plan is no longer every step a human's on H0** (NT1, #583 ; `docs/operations.md`, « L'exécuteur d'identité », geste 6). `docker-compose.yml` passed none of the variables of `eregion_core/never_target.py` to the watch: on H0 the list was unreadable (no operator named), `covers()` held every id, and each verb of an identity plan (IR1a) became a human's step — closed, but the plan lost its use. Now the watch, and it alone, receives `EREGION_OPERATOR_OBJECT_ID`, `EREGION_PROTECTED_IDENTITIES` and `EREGION_IDENTITY_EXECUTOR_CLIENT_ID` (neither the console nor the bot reads the list; the injectors have theirs in `orodruin.env`). `infra/glorfindel-host/host_env.py` copies them from the operator's environment into `host.env`, each one's source a Terraform output: `operator_object_id` and `protected_identities` of `infra/orodruin`, `identity_executor_client_id` and `identity_executor_object_id` of `infra/glorfindel-host` (`host.env.example`). Writing `host.env` and `--check` (run by `make glorfindel-host-push`) say a variable left empty, or an entry that is no object id, with its source and never its value — and never refuse: an unreadable list stays a refusal of the verbs, not a breakdown.
  - Tests : `tests/unit/test_glorfindel_host.py`.
