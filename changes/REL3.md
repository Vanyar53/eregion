### Fixed
- **`make release-draft` judges the release gate by its jobs, never by the run's conclusion** (REL3, #569 ; `docs/operations.md`, « La porte de release »). At the 0.5.6 (05/11), macOS, cancelled for want of a runner, set the run to `failure` while the total « release gate » and every job that blocks were green, and the draft was refused — against H1c's rule that macOS informs and never blocks. Once the run is found and complete, its jobs are read (one `GET …/actions/runs/{id}/jobs?filter=latest`): the total present, complete and green; every job that blocks green, even beside a green total; of a job re-run, its latest attempt stands; a job that informs (macOS) red, cancelled or absent is said after the green line, never refusing. A read that fails, an answer that is not a list of jobs, a full page, a job without a name refuse. The jobs that inform have one source: `INFORMATIVE` and `GATE_JOB` in `scripts/release.py`, read against `release-gate.yml` (the total's `INFORMATIVE`, each job's name, the total's `needs`, `continue-on-error`).
  - Tests : `tests/unit/test_release.py` (the fake GitHub gives the workflow's own jobs, each matrix expanded) ; mutants M4035–M4039 (`scripts/mutants/REL3.py`), and CI6's M3593 now aimed at the total job.
