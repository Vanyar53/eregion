### Fixed
- **The reassertion of a block reads the status its write returns: AWS's `failed` is never counted as put back** (RW1, #513; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La repose lit ce que répond le fournisseur »). On AWS, `block_suspicious_ip` answers `{"status": "failed"}` without raising when no ACL entry can be placed, and `{"status": "partial"}` when an entry does not hold at its read-back. Both times of the reassertion (the perimeter pair, then the entry) now read it. `failed` is a failure: the card says the put-back failed, with the provider's reason, and `reasserted_at` is not written, so the block can be tried again. Before, the card said « ses règles réécrites une fois » of rules never written, and the block was never tried again. `partial` is a put-back that does not hold, whatever the re-read says. Before, the entry was marked `partial` with no card: the second time skipped it, held by its own mark. An entry the provider marked `partial` is read by no later pass until it is blocked again whole or unblocked, and its card says so instead of « Glorfindel alertera ». Azure is unchanged: a partial VM block raises (`PartialActionError`), and a perimeter's `blocked_partial` is left to the re-read.
