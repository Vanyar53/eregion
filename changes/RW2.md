### Fixed
- **The reassertion of an isolation reads what its write answered: an isolation that does not hold is never « reposée une fois »** (RW2, #555 ; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Réaffirmation »). On AWS, `isolate_vm` answers `isolated` with `verified` False or None and the ENIs in `bypass`; on Azure, a deny written behind a customer's allow answers `isolated` with `bypass`. Both made the card say « reposée une fois. Si elle disparaît encore, Glorfindel alertera », and `reasserted_at` took the isolation out of every later put-back. Now the put-back is `partial`: the card says it does not hold, with the provider's words, promises nothing, and `reasserted_at` is not set.
  - Tests : `tests/unit/test_reassert_verified.py`, `tests/unit/test_invariant_regressions.py`.
- **A perimeter pair the first time of the reassertion wrote partial is said, even when its second time never comes** (RW2, #555). The first time marks the entry `partial_unsaid` before `reasserted_at`; the second time takes it off once the card is said. Skipped (the VM's lock held, the pass cut off), the next pass reads the entry and says it, writing nothing (the mode may have changed since) — before, the provider's `partial` mark held it out of every pass, its card never written. A block written whole takes the mark off.
  - Tests : `tests/unit/test_reassert_verified.py`.
- **The card of a write the provider said partial, re-read whole, says « le fournisseur l'a dit partiel : à vérifier »**, no longer « une écriture a échoué » (RW2, #555). Azure's `PartialActionError` on a put-back, and `_held_partial`'s sentence on its card, are fixed by a test.
  - Tests : `tests/unit/test_reassert_verified.py`.
