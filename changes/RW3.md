### Fixed
- **The put-back of an isolation reads its effect again: « reposée une fois » only of an isolation the cloud, read again, holds** (RW3, #575 ; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Réaffirmation »). After `isolate_vm`, the reassertion reads the isolation again (`verify_isolation`), as the put-back of a block does (A0). On Azure, each deny is confirmed at its write, never what holds once everything is written: a rule taken off between two writes (an apply in a loop), an allow placed after the write's plan, a read refused — the card said « reposée une fois. Si elle disparaît encore, Glorfindel alertera ». Now a re-read that does not hold (« relue, elle ne tient pas », with the NICs left uncovered or the reason) or cannot be read (`verified` None, or a read that raises: « relue, elle n'est pas lisible ») is a put-back that does not hold (`partial`): the card says it after the provider's words, if any, and promises nothing. RW2's « once » applies: `reasserted_at` set (the original `isolated_at` kept), `drift_alerted_at` after the card; gone again, « une deuxième fois », escalated alone, nothing written. A bypass the re-read finds gets its `bypassed` card in the same pass, after the put-back's card. On AWS, the re-read comes on top of the adapter's own read-back: an ENI put back in its groups in between is said.
  - Cost, measured: one `verify_isolation` more per put-back — as many reads as the check before it (on Azure 1 + 4 per NIC for an isolation by rules: 5 for one NIC, 9 for two; 2 on AWS); none on a pass where everything holds, nor in `human_only`. No write more: the fake Azure's journal of writes is unchanged.
  - The invariant machine's I3 now also judges the reassertion's isolation cards: « reposée une fois » is never said of a VM a flow still reaches (`tests/unit/test_invariants.py`).
  - Tests : `tests/unit/test_reassert_reread.py`, `tests/unit/test_reassert.py`, `tests/unit/test_reassert_verified.py`.
