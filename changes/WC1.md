### Security
- **The return channel names who writes it: H0's watch by default, or a workstation's or a team's** (WC1, #523; `docs/design/modele-de-menace.md`, §5). Each identity listed gets exactly what H0's gets — `Storage Blob Data Reader` on `exercises`, `Storage Blob Data Contributor` on `watch`, at the container's scope, a service principal —, never Orodruin's own identity nor the operator's who applies (both refused at the plan). Annatar's, Terraform does not know: the operator names the identity. The heartbeat stays signed by the watch's key, pinned in `orodruin.env`: another key is still refused (SEC19). The detections are not signed yet: every identity that writes `watch` can skew a run's verdict, `detected` as well as `missed` — the measure, never an action, since an injector acts only on the signed heartbeat. Signing them is planned (SEC21).
